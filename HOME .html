FAQ

Common questions

Yes, booking is recommended so we can confirm your visit and allocate the right amount of time for your consultation or treatment.

Do you treat new patients?

Absolutely. We welcome new patients and offer friendly consultations to help you start your dental care journey.

Are prices fixed?

Prices depend on the treatment, the time required, and the patient’s specific needs, so we provide treatment-based estimates during consultation.

Yes, we offer same-day emergency support where possible to help patients in urgent dental pain or discomfort.
